    The sport

    Hybrid racing

    Running plus functional strength, raced against the clock. It's one of the fastest-growing formats in fitness, and it's where endurance and power meet.

    HYROX

    8 km run + 8 stations

    Eight 1 km runs, each followed by a workout station: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, and wall balls.

    Spartan DEKA

    10 zones, one clock

    Ten functional fitness zones. DEKA FIT adds a 500 m run between each zone, DEKA MILE uses shorter runs, and DEKA STRONG is the zones only.
